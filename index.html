Please Pay Special Attention

  1. PatPatDots has no account system. You do not need to register or sign in, and we do not collect your phone number, email address, name, government ID number, bank card number, or biometric data.
  2. All data you generate while using the app (training records, bean profiles, preferences) is stored locally on your phone. It is never uploaded to our servers. We can neither see it nor obtain it.
  3. The app needs Bluetooth permission to connect to PatPatDots devices. Bluetooth scanning is declared as not used to derive location (neverForLocation). We do not infer your location.
  4. If you are a child under 14, please read this policy with your guardian and use this app only with your guardian's consent.

2. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

2.1 We follow the principle of data minimisation: beyond what is necessary to provide the features, we collect nothing else. We do not collect information that identifies you personally, and we do not read your contacts, messages, photo library, microphone, or camera.

2.2 The following information is created by you inside the app and stored only on your device, to show you your training activity and personal preferences:

  • Training and interaction records: session counts, duration, scores, reaction times, per-game progress and preferences;
  • Bean profiles: the names you give your beans, chosen or custom avatars, creation time, and interaction statistics;
  • Personalisation settings: language, care mode, demo mode, vibration and brightness, in-game music and vibration toggles;
  • Bluetooth device information: the name, identifier, and signal strength of recently connected devices, used for faster reconnection.

2.3 Bluetooth device identifiers: to remember the PatPatDots devices you have connected, your phone stores that device's Bluetooth identifier (on Android, its MAC address). This is a device identifier rather than personal identity information, it stays on your device, and it is never uploaded. You can delete the history from the device list.

2.4 Third-party information you enter yourself: in the custom music feature you may type a track title and artist name. You provide this yourself, so please do not enter anyone else's private information.

2.5 We do not collect precise or approximate location information.

2.6 We process no health data of any kind. Training scores and trends in the app are not medical test results and cannot be used to determine whether you have any medical condition.

3. How We Store Your Personal Information

3.1 Location: all data is stored locally on your phone, in app preference storage and a local event database (patpatdots_events.db). We do not store your data on any server.

3.2 Retention: data is kept for as long as you use the app, until you delete it in the app, clear local data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all local data.

3.3 Cross-border transfer: because data never leaves your device, there is no transfer of personal information across borders, and we do not provide your personal information to recipients outside your country.

4. How We Protect Your Personal Information

4.1 Because data does not leave your device, its security depends mainly on the security of the device itself. We recommend setting a screen lock, keeping your operating system up to date, and avoiding use on jailbroken or rooted devices.

4.2 Bluetooth communication takes place directly between the device and your phone and does not pass over the internet.

4.3 We process information only to the extent necessary to provide the features, and we reduce risk through permission isolation and minimising what is stored locally.

4.4 Although we take reasonable security measures, please understand that no technical measure is beyond being breached. If a personal information security incident unfortunately occurs, we will promptly inform you by push notification or announcement of what happened, the possible impact, and the measures we have taken.

5. How We Share, Transfer, or Publicly Disclose Your Personal Information

5.1 We do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose any of your personal information.

5.2 The app integrates no third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, profiling, or attribution SDK. You are welcome to verify the dependency list inside the installed package.

5.3 We may provide necessary information only where required by law, or in response to a request made by a judicial or administrative authority through due legal process. Where the law permits, we will inform you of such a request.

5.4 If a merger, split, or transfer of assets occurs, we will require the successor to remain bound by this policy; otherwise we will seek your consent again.

6. Your Rights

6.1 Under China's Personal Information Protection Law you have the right to be informed, to decide, to access, to copy, to correct, to supplement, to delete, and to withdraw consent in relation to your personal information. Because all data is stored on your device and is not linked to your identity, you can exercise the following rights directly in the app without contacting us:

  • Access and copy: view all your data under "Mine", "Settings", and the relevant feature pages;
  • Correct and complete: change bean names and avatars, and adjust any setting;
  • Delete: remove individual training records, a single bean profile, or Bluetooth device history, or clear everything at once with "Clear local data";
  • Withdraw consent: turn off any permission, or stop using the relevant feature.

6.2 You may also contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond within fifteen working days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to a regulator or bring an action before a competent people's court.

6.3 Because the app has no accounts, there is no "delete account" action; uninstalling the app removes all local data.

7. Protection of Minors

7.1 If you are a child under 14, please read this policy together with your guardian and use this app only with your guardian's consent.

7.2 We do not actively collect personal information from children. A guardian may contact us using the details in section 1 to exercise rights of access, correction, or deletion on a child's behalf.

7.3 Guardians should fulfil their supervisory duties, guide children in using the app, and pay attention to usage time and eye health.
